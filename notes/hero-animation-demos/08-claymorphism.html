Claymorphism

Friendly, rounded, toy-like depth. Motion feels soft, responsive, and slightly exaggerated.

Strategy A: Spring-pop entrance

Hello, little helper

A cheerful way to keep track of chores.

Sequence
Main illustration or card pops into view, headline follows, CTA springs into place.
Motion
Scale and opacity with spring-like easing and restrained overshoot.
Use when
Friendly illustrations, avatars, rounded UI cards.

Strategy B: Floating character or object

Play lives here

Soft shapes that float while you read.

Sequence
Main content appears, decorative objects begin a subtle floating loop, pointer interaction adds a small response.
Motion
Slow vertical transforms in a loop, pointer-linked translate per object depth. Move the pointer over the stage.
Use when
Hero needs personality and warmth.

Strategy C: Squash-and-settle

Land softly

Sequence
A main object lands in place, compresses slightly, springs back to its resting shape, CTA receives attention.
Motion
Scale keyframes anchored at the object's base, applied to the object only.
Use when
Toy-like buttons, soft 3D illustrations, playful product visuals.

Avoid: Excessive overshoot and simultaneous bouncing. One or two expressive elements are usually enough.
