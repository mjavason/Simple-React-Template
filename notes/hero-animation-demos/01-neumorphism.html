Neumorphism

Tactile softness. Motion makes elements feel pressed, raised, or gently emerging from a soft surface.

Strategy A: Soft emergence

Soft by design

A calm surface where every control feels within reach.

Sequence
Headline fades in and rises slightly, supporting copy follows, CTA gently emerges last.
Motion
opacity 0 to 1, y 12 to 0, short stagger.
Use when
Typography-led hero that needs a calm, polished entrance.

Strategy B: Tactile press-and-release

ThermostatTap a control
Sequence
Elements start in a sunken state, the main visual rises, CTA responds to hover and press with changing shadows.
Motion
Animated shadow values and a very small scale change. Hover and press the CTA after the entrance.
Use when
Floating controls, rounded panels, tactile product mockup.

Strategy C: Layered soft reveal

Layers of calm

Sequence
Recessed background panel appears, foreground cards rise at slightly different intervals, headline settles into view.
Motion
Small vertical translations, opacity, stagger with uneven intervals.
Use when
Hero with multiple layered UI elements.

Avoid: Large displacement or exaggerated bouncing. Neumorphism depends on subtle differences in light and depth.
