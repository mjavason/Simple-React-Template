Brutalism

Confrontation, contrast, and deliberate visual disruption. Motion can be abrupt, oversized, or unconventional.

Strategy A: Hard-cut typography

NO RULES.
JUST THE POINT.

A studio for work that refuses to blend in.

Sequence
Oversized heading appears in large blocks with no easing, secondary text follows, CTA snaps into view.
Motion
Instant opacity cuts at timed delays, short decisive scale snap on CTA.
Use when
Typography is the central visual statement.

Strategy B: Directional collision

IN YOUR FACE
NO WAY AROUND

COLLIDE.

Sequence
Two graphic elements enter from opposing directions, stop abruptly in a composed arrangement, headline becomes the focal point.
Motion
Large but short translations with sharp easing.
Use when
Overlapping blocks, strong borders, confrontational compositions.

Strategy C: Chaotic-to-ordered reveal

BREAKTHEGRID

Then put it back, on purpose.

Sequence
Typography and graphic fragments appear at different positions, align into a deliberate composition, CTA becomes clear.
Motion
Staggered transforms with selective rotation.
Use when
Experimental brand where controlled disorder supports the concept.

Avoid: Confusing randomness with expression. The result still needs a clear focal point and a readable message.
