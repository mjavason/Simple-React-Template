Glassmorphism

Transparency, depth, and light. Motion makes elements feel suspended between layers.

Strategy A: Floating glass panels

Clear view of everything

One translucent surface for all your numbers.

Sequence
Background gradient is visible, glass panel fades and moves upward, heading and CTA appear inside it.
Motion
Opacity, small y translation, stagger.
Use when
Floating dashboard, translucent card, product panel.

Strategy C: Ambient light drift

Always in motion, barely

The light moves. The content stays still.

Sequence
Hero appears almost fully formed, blurred shapes slowly drift behind the content, foreground panel responds subtly to pointer movement.
Motion
Slow transforms on background elements, pointer-linked translate. Move the pointer over the stage.
Use when
Atmospheric, futuristic hero without a dramatic entrance.

Avoid: Continuously animating large backdrop-filter regions. Animate the elements behind the glass instead where possible.
