Dark Mode Aesthetics

Controlled contrast, luminous accents, and visual depth. Motion makes important elements emerge from darkness without compromising readability. The tenth category from the brief, Brutalist, uses the Brutalism page.

Motion

Strategy B: Ambient background emergence

Signal in the dark

Monitoring that stays quiet until it matters.

Sequence
Background gradients emerge, hero content fades in, background continues moving very slowly.
Motion
Opacity and transforms on separate background layers.
Use when
Atmospheric, technical, or futuristic design.
